<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Parent document → child window | A second document renders the owner's session data |
| Child window lifetime → the session | A stale or orphaned window must never hold a live connection of its own |
| Browser pop-up policy → the app | The browser may refuse, and the app must degrade plainly |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Severity | Component | Disposition | Mitigation |
|-----------|----------|----------|-----------|-------------|------------|
| T-5-37 | Information Disclosure | Medium | a pop-out opening a second authenticated surface, or a cross-origin window reading session data | mitigate | `window.open('')` creates a blank same-origin document with no URL and no router; nothing new is authenticated, no route is added, and `postMessage` is not used |
| T-5-38 | Denial of Service (orphaned resources) | Medium | a popped-out window outliving the play screen and holding a subscription | mitigate | One subscription for the whole panel; a `useEffect` cleanup closes every child window on unmount or connection change; `beforeunload` on the child clears the parent's handle |
| T-5-39 | Tampering (half-moved state) | Low | a blocked pop-up leaving a view neither docked nor windowed | mitigate | `openPopout` returns `null` on a block and the caller stays docked and shows the locked notice |
| T-5-40 | Spoofing (unstyled or look-alike window) | Low | a child window rendering unstyled and being mistaken for something else | mitigate | Every stylesheet and style node is cloned into the child head; the window title names AI-player or AI-chatter and the connection |
| T-5-SC | Tampering (supply chain) | Low | `frontend/package.json` | accept | This plan installs nothing; `createPortal` is already inside the installed `react-dom` |
</threat_model>
