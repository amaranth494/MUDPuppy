<objective>
Give the owner a stop button for AI-player that means what it says, and make the stream say so. Today the switch has three positions and exactly one memory of why it is waiting — a disconnect. A pause built on that would be undone by the next reconnect, because `resumeAutopilotLocked` maps WAITING to ON unconditionally. And a pause nobody can see in the stream is a pause the owner cannot prove happened.

Purpose: D-13 puts Pause/Resume on AI-player as one instant, model-call-free control, and D-15 says the badge stays three states while the server tracks *why* it is waiting, resumes only when every reason has cleared, and **the thinking stream prints a notice on pause and on resume**. D-14 says a paused AI-player still reads the game so the first decision after resume reassesses rather than replaying a stale plan.
Output: two independent waiting reasons on the autopilot record, two new Manager methods that fire the engage hook only when both are clear, two new actions on the existing autopilot endpoint, the reasons on the wire for the panel, the two locked thinking-stream notices, and the tests that make all of it falsifiable.

No prompt and no model call in this plan, and no change to any file under `internal/driver`. Pause stops the loop through the hook Phase 4 already fires for a wheel-grab, and the notices ride the AI system-line channel that already exists.
</objective>
