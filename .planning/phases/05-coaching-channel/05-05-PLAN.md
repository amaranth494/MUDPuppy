<objective>
Give the owner someone to ask. Today the panel can show what AI-player decided and why, but there is no way to ask it anything — not "why did you do that", not "what do you know about this quest", not "which rule is stopping you". D-17 says AI-chatter reads everything AI-player knows, read-only, and D-11 says it always answers in short plain text.

Purpose: this plan builds the conversation itself — the second, independent model call that sits above the play loop, the storage that keeps it beside the decisions it was about, and the message channel that carries it. It is deliberately the whole of the *answering* half of D-01: the owner can hold a conversation about the play and get real answers grounded in everything the player model sees.

The *coaching* half — pushing a line down so it changes the next decision, and taking it back in words — is plan 05-06, which extends this same call's answer shape and this same instruction with the push and withdraw job, and adds the coaching currently in effect to this plan's `chatPromptContext`. That is a split of one capability into two shippable ones, not a reduced version of either: nothing in this plan is a stub, a placeholder or a first cut, and plan 05-06 replaces nothing built here.

Output: migration 015, conversation storage, a third `gemini` entry point, `HandleChat` with a prompt that carries everything AI-player knows plus a bounded tail of the conversation so far, the chat websocket message in both directions, and the hook wiring.
</objective>
