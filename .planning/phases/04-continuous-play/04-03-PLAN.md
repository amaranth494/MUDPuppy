<objective>
Turn one decision into continuous play. Phase 3's `HandleEngage` fires once per `#AUTO ON` and then autopilot sits on and idle; this plan makes it the first iteration of a paced, cancellable loop that keeps reading, deciding and acting until something stops it — and makes "something stops it" immediate rather than eventual.

Purpose: REQ-continuous-loop's "the loop runs read, decide, act continuously against a live game, pacing itself to the game's turn rhythm rather than flooding it", plus the half of REQ-doc-wheel-grab-and-reengage the existing wheel-grab does not cover. The command path already disengages instantly when the owner types; what does not exist is anything that tells the *driver* to stop, so a loop asleep in its pacing wait — or waiting on a model call that can take up to two minutes — would keep a ghost decision alive after the owner believes he has the wheel back. A symmetric `DisengageHook` is the fix (04-RESEARCH Pattern 2).
Output: `EngageLoop`/`StopLoop` and the pacing goroutine; an output-arrived signal and a disengage hook on the session manager; both hooks rewired in `main.go`; and the pacing, reassess, wheel-grab and waiting tests.

Also closes D-27 (DR-3-07): the dead statement in `EngageAutopilot`, deleted here because this is the first plan that touches `internal/session/manager.go`.

Counters, thresholds, retries and block counting are **not** in this plan — they are 04-04. This plan's loop runs until the switch leaves on, and nothing else stops it yet.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| The autopilot switch → the loop goroutine | The switch is the authorization; a loop that outlives it is acting without one |
| The game socket → the loop's wakeups | Game output decides when the AI acts, and game output is attacker-controlled text |
| The loop → the owner's model quota | Every iteration spends calls on a free-tier key |
| The loop → the MUD | Commands issued too fast are indistinguishable from flooding, which the policy forbids |
