<objective>
Give the owner a place to say what he wants done. Until now the AI has had conduct rules and approach guidance — standing direction — and nothing that says what this session is for. D-01 puts a one-line goal box at the top of the AI Assist panel, stored server-side against the connection profile, surviving refreshes, reconnects and the switch going off and on, and lasting until the owner changes it.

The goal is also the name of a Quest. D-04 makes typing a goal create a Quest record for it, or reactivate an open one whose goal text matches, so the progress the AI accumulates against a goal outlives the session it was made in. Phase 4 creates and reactivates Quests and reads them; it never closes one — closure and promotion into Historical Memory are Phase 6's work, by the memory model's own design.

Purpose: REQ-continuous-loop's "the owner sets a session goal", and the storage every later memory plan hangs off. `04-UI-SPEC.md` locks the box's label, placeholder, hint, save behaviour and both system lines.
Output: migration 013, the goal field through the profile store, a Quest store, the goal sub-resource with its ownership check, and the goal box in the panel.

The goal does not reach the model in this plan — plan 04-07 puts it in the prompt. What this plan delivers is the owner's ability to set it, see it, change it during play, and have it still be there tomorrow.
</objective>
