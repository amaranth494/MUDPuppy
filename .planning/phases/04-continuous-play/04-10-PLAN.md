<objective>
Make Phase 4's reachable half provable by one command a reader can run, and prove the prover before trusting it.

The owner's evidence rule allows exactly two kinds of proof: a canned report and a screenshot. Four of this phase's surfaces are HTTP-reachable and therefore belong in a report rather than in a screenshot caption — the goal round trip and its Quest, the Session Memory read-back, the retention delete with its counts, and the ownership refusal on all three. The loop's own pacing, the cap and threshold halts and the re-engage reassessment are not reachable by an HTTP client at all; they are proven by the Go suite and by the walkthrough screenshots, and this harness must print them as SKIP lines naming that proof rather than pretending to check them. Phase 3's review recorded the lesson that made this rule: a harness that cannot fail is not evidence, which is why a negative self-test ships beside it.

Purpose: the canned-report half of the ROADMAP's Phase Validation line, and `04-VALIDATION.md`'s requirement that the goal round trip, the memory read-back and the retention counts are proven through the HTTP surface and the log line, never by a query.
Output: `scripts/verify-phase4.sh` with its invocation modes, and both fixture sets.

This plan runs no live Gemini call, deploys nothing, and changes no data beyond the profile the caller names — which it puts back as it found it.
</objective>
