<objective>
Put the AI on the screen. A floating, minimizable AI Assist panel appears on the play screen for any profile with the AI activated, shows each decision as a short reasoning followed by the command it issued, shows state changes and failures as system lines, rebuilds itself from the server after a refresh, and has no message box — that is Phase 5's. In the terminal, the AI's command prints as `[AI-ASSIST > north]` so a typed command and an AI-issued one can never be mistaken for each other.

Purpose: REQ-reasoning-visibility is an owner-facing claim and this is the surface that makes it true. D-06 to D-10 are the owner's own description of what he wants to see: "if you have activated the AI assist from the settings on a profile, you get a pop-up chat interface with the AI model where you can see its reasoning", and "`[AI-ASSIST > north]` — and that should substitute for local echo to differentiate between typed commands."
Output: one new component, its CSS, the two new colour tokens, the websocket and fetch plumbing, and the play screen's mount and terminal line.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Severity | Component | Disposition | Mitigating Task |
