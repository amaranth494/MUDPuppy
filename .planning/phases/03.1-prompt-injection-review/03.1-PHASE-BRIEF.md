## Phase Goal

Game text, including other players' speech and hostile room descriptions, cannot steer the AI into issuing a command the owner would not sanction. Three layers go in behind the existing shape validation: a hardened prompt (game text delimited and declared untrusted), an owner-editable Never-issue list on the profile enforced in Go before dispatch, and a reviewer pass (a second Gemini call with a constrained verdict). A block sends nothing, leaves autopilot ON and idle, and shows the owner the command and the reason.
