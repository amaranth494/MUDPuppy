# MUDPuppy AI Player: Safety and Abuse Policy

Policy version: 1.0
Date: 2026-09-14

This policy must be accepted for each game profile before the AI player can be engaged on that profile. Accepting it is a statement that you, the profile owner, take responsibility for what the AI does on that game under your account. Acceptance is recorded once per profile with timestamp and policy version; it does not expire and is not re-asked when the policy changes.

## 1. Respect the game's rules

- Before engaging the AI on a profile, you must confirm that the game behind that profile permits automated play. You are responsible for knowing that game's rules on bots, scripting, and automation, and for keeping up with changes to them.
- Do not engage the AI on a game that prohibits automation. The AI player will not be used to conceal automated play from a game's operators or to defeat their bot detection.
- Where a game permits automation with conditions (restricted areas, attended play only, behavior requirements), those conditions must be entered into the profile's conduct rules before the AI is engaged, and they bind the AI's play.

## 2. Supervise the AI

- The AI plays under your supervision. You are expected to be reachable while it plays and to check on its behavior at reasonable intervals.
- Do not leave the AI running unattended for extended periods. If you need to step away for long, disengage it.
- If the AI is disconnected or kicked from a game while engaged, it will not reconnect on its own, and you must not re-engage it until you understand why it was disconnected.

## 3. Respect the people in the game

- The games this tool connects to are shared worlds with real people in them. The AI must not be used to harass, deceive, impersonate, or interfere with other players, to camp or drain shared resources in ways the game community considers abusive, or to gain unfair advantage in player-versus-player situations.
- If a game's staff or players raise a concern about the AI's behavior, disengage first and discuss second.

## 4. Respect the game's infrastructure

- The AI must operate within this tool's rate and safety limits. Do not raise a profile's limits to levels that could flood a game server with commands or connections.
- One AI-driven character per game at a time under this profile, unless the game's rules expressly allow more.

## 5. Own the costs

- AI play consumes paid model API calls under your API key. The per-session call cap exists to protect you; raising it is your choice and your cost.

## 6. Credentials and data

- Game credentials stored in a profile are used only to connect that profile. The AI does not use stored credentials for any other purpose.
- Game text captured during play, including other players' words, is stored in session logs and learned notes for this tool's operation. Do not use it to profile or target other players.

## 7. Enforcement

- Violating this policy is grounds for disabling AI features on the profile involved, or on the account.
- Nothing in this policy overrides the connected game's own rules or applicable law. Where they conflict, the stricter rule applies.
